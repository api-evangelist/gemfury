We’ve been quiet on changes the last few months because we have been repaying technical and operational debt. The biggest by far was migrating from our custom “Stackfury” orchestration solution to Kubernetes. Although these changes are not immediately visible to you as a customer, this is a big win for reliability, performance, and flexibility in our future roadmap.
