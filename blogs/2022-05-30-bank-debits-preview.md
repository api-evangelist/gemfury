We consistently receive requests to use US bank payments to pay for Gemfury subscriptions, and we’ve long offered a manual path via invoiced billing. Today, we’re opening the preview for automated bank payments. This new feature offers a closer experience to credit card subscriptions where our payment processor stores your information and periodically debits your subscription payments without intervention.
