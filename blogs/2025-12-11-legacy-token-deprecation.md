We are continuing the transition away from full-access tokens to purpose-specific tokens. Recently, we’ve taken the following steps based on your feedback: Stronger deprecation messaging & notifications Enabled permanent removal of full-access tokens Added option to block full-access tokens Enabled collaborator-created tokens We will discuss these updates in more detail in future posts.
