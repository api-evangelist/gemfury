Following last week’s MFA enhancements , we’ve added an email confirmation option for those who haven’t yet enabled a stronger authentication factor. When updating sensitive settings, you can now confirm your identity with a one-time code sent to your account email. For better security, we still encourage you to set up a passkey or an authenticator app.
