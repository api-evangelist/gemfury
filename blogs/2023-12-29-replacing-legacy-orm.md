Over the past few months, we have successfully completed a migration from a legacy ORM to a more contemporary solution. This upgrade has brought us the advantages of a modern ORM, while also enabling us to advance several other dependencies. Although these improvements may not be immediately apparent to you as a customer, they have notably enhanced reliability and performance, ensuring our commitment to continue delivering high-quality services.
